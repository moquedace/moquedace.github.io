Bio

A bilingual short profile for academic, technical and collaborative contexts.

English

Cassio Marques Moquedace dos Santos is a soil scientist and postdoctoral researcher at ESALQ-USP, working with the GeoCiS group in the Department of Soil Science. His research combines digital soil mapping, pedometrics, machine learning and soil organic carbon modelling to understand soil-landscape systems and environmental change.

Português

Cassio Marques Moquedace dos Santos e cientista do solo e pesquisador de pos-doutorado na ESALQ-USP, atuando no GeoCiS, Departamento de Ciencia do Solo. Sua pesquisa integra mapeamento digital de solos, pedometria, aprendizado de maquina e modelagem de carbono organico do solo para compreender sistemas solo-paisagem e mudancas ambientais.
